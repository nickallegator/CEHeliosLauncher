# AG Launcher 2.10.0-test.1

This release rebuilds Allegator Games News as a responsive editorial workspace that fits the launcher’s workshop identity.

## Changes

- Replaces the legacy article carousel with a persistent archive and focused reader.
- Adds fast local search and dynamic category filters across the current News feed.
- Introduces responsive article banners, readable typography, and layouts for large and minimum-size windows.
- Preserves article, filter, archive-scroll, and reading positions while navigating during a launcher session.
- Keeps JSON-first loading, RSS fallback, safe links, offline caching, unread state, and Home article deep links.
- Adds deterministic Electron visual coverage for wide, minimum, and compact drawer layouts.

Test releases remain unsigned while Windows Public Trust signing is prepared.
