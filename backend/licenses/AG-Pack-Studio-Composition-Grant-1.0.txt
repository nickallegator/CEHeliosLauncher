AG Pack Studio Composition Grant 1.0

By enabling Pack Studio composition for a Resource Pack revision, the publisher
grants recipients permission to copy unchanged resources from that revision and
to perform the mechanical JSON aggregation required to combine those resources
with other opted-in Resource Packs for use with Minecraft, Cobblemon, Cobble
Power, and Allegator Games Community content.

Every generated pack must retain the creator attribution, the original license,
this composition grant, and included third-party notices. Copyright and all
rights not expressly granted remain with their respective owners. Disabling the
grant prevents future Pack Studio builds but does not revoke packs that were
already lawfully generated.
